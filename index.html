Quiet Time
How to Use Quiet Time
↗ Open Full Page

1. Quick Start (60-Second Daily Flow)

Quiet Time is a private devotional workspace for unhurried Bible meditation. Everything you write stays safely in your browser.

  • Follow the Rhythm: Open 🌿 7 Steps Guide in the top bar for the daily quiet-time sequence.
  • Load a Passage: Click 🔍 Change Verse, enter any passage (e.g., Psalm 23:1-3), choose a translation, and press Enter.
  • Reflect & Record: Tap any word in the verse to study its meaning, answer questions on the left, and record revelations in Notes on the right.

2. Scripture Desk, Quick Passages & Word Study

  • 11+ Translations & Paste: Switch between KJV, BSB, WEB, ASV, YLT, and more—or click 📋 Paste to study any custom translation.
  • Smart Quick Passages: Inside 🔍 Change Verse, Quick Passages automatically reads and adds verses you mention or insert in your Notes—and resets cleanly when you reset your notes.
  • Interactive Word Study: Click any word in the scripture to view live definitions, open 📖 Concordance, get a ✨ Word Meaning hint, or insert the definition into your notes.
  • Multi-Verse Badges: Click numbered verse badges (e.g., v.2) to focus questions and Study Hints on a specific verse.

3. The Three Bible Study Modes

  • 🔬 Microscopic (13 Questions): Deep word-by-word reflection with ✨ Study Hint (auto-reloads whenever your selected verse changes) and one-click insertion into notes.
  • 🏷️ Topical (Six W's): Study biblical themes like Faith, Patience, Love, or Zeal. Typing a topic auto-loads its definition into your study.
  • 📜 Telescopic (Whole-Book): Map a whole book's author, context, and central theme across all 66 books, with 📖 Load Passage to jump straight into chapter 1.

4. Formatting, Highlights & Private Spellcheck

  • Live Rich Editor: Bold (Ctrl+B), Italic (Ctrl+I), Underline (Ctrl+U), 5 highlight colors (Ctrl+H), headings, bullets, and Undo/Redo.
  • ✓ Spellcheck: Toggle private offline spellcheck (55,000+ English & Biblical words) in the toolbar. Click any red-underlined word for suggestions or to add it to your personal dictionary.

5. WhatsApp Sharing & A4 Print Sheets

  • 💬 Copy for WhatsApp: Formats headings, *bold*, _italic_, bullets, and highlights (as WhatsApp `shaded pills`) cleanly—with zero raw Markdown clutter.
  • 🖨️ Print Sheet / Save PDF: Prints your notes cleanly onto standard A4 paper with natural multi-page flow and full-page theme background coverage (accessible from ⋯ → 📖 Full Document Sheet, ⋯ → 🖨️ Print / Save PDF, or Ctrl+P).
Tip for Dark & Sepia PDFs: In your browser's print window, expand More settings and check Background graphics so the full A4 paper color prints edge-to-edge.

6. Archive, Snapshots, Privacy & Multi-Device Sync

  • 📁 Archive & 🕘 Snapshots: Archive finished studies for safekeeping, or recover earlier drafts from ⋯ → 🕘 Restore Snapshots.
  • Storage & Sync: Private to this device by default. Open ⋯ → Storage & Sync to pair your phone & laptop with a private sync key or export a JSON backup.
  • Reset Controls: ↺ Reset All resets only the current study tab; ⋯ → 🧹 Hard Reset clears all local browser data for a fresh start.
