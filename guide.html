How to Use Quiet Time

Quiet Time is a calm, private devotional studio inspired by Bishop Dag Heward-Mills' teaching on How You Can Have an Effective Quiet Time with God Every Day. Everything you write stays privately in your browser unless you choose to sync or share it.

Your Daily Rhythm in 60 Seconds

  1. Open the 7 Steps Guide: Click 🌿 7 Steps Guide in the top bar for the gentle devotional sequence—from opening prayer to recording revelations and concluding in prayer.
  2. Choose a Passage: Click 🔍 Change Verse on the Scripture Desk, type any reference (such as Psalm 23:1-3 or John 3:16), pick a translation, and press Enter.
  3. Meditate & Reflect: Tap any word in the verse to see its definition, or step through the 13 Microscopic questions on the left.
  4. Record Revelations: Write directly in the Notes Studio on the right—or click + Insert on any verse, definition, or reflection answer to drop it neatly into your journal.
  5. Save, Share, or Print: Notes autosave continuously. Archive completed sessions, copy them cleanly for WhatsApp, or print them onto A4 paper.

Loading Verses, Quick Passages & Word Study

  • Search Any Passage: Click 🔍 Change Verse and enter a verse or range (e.g., Romans 8:28-30). Choose from 11+ open Bible translations (KJV, BSB, WEB, ASV, YLT, and more) or paste your own custom passage with 📋 Paste.
  • Smart Quick Passages: Inside the verse shelf, Quick Passages starts with 5 foundational scriptures and automatically reads and adds any Bible verses you mention or insert in your Notes. When you reset your notes, those added chips reset cleanly too.
  • BibleHub Parallel: Click 🌐 BibleHub Parallel to compare 30+ translations side by side in a new tab.
  • Click Any Word to Study It: Every word in the displayed scripture is interactive. Click a word to view its live dictionary meaning, open 📖 Concordance (Strong's lookup), request a short ✨ Word Meaning hint, or insert the word study directly into your notes.
  • Multi-Verse Focusing: When you load a range of verses, click any numbered badge (such as v.2) to focus your reflection questions and Study Hints on that exact verse.

The Three Bible Study Modes

🔬 Microscopic Study (13 Analytical Questions)

Slow down and examine a single verse or short passage word by word through 13 hermeneutical questions—identifying God's character, commands to obey, promises to claim, warnings to heed, and personal applications.

  • Target Verse Selector: Switch between the whole passage, individual verses, or other verses referenced in your notes.
  • ✨ Study Hint: Opens a brief verse-specific reflection nudge. Whenever you switch to a different verse, an open Study Hint reloads automatically for the newly selected verse.
  • One-Click Insertion: Insert a single answer, all written answers (+ Insert All Answers), the full Q&A outline (+ Insert All Q&A), or break a multi-verse passage into headings (+ Outline All Verses).

🏷️ Topical Study (The Six W's)

Explore a biblical theme—such as Patience, Faith, Love, or Zeal—across Scripture. Typing any topic automatically looks up its definition, and the Six W's (Why, How, Where, When, What, Who) guide your synthesis into its own dedicated Topical Notes draft.

📜 Telescopic Study (Whole-Book Overview)

Step back to grasp the big picture of an entire Bible book: pick any of the 66 books, record the author, historical setting, recipients, and overarching theme, click 📖 Load Passage to bring chapter 1 onto the Scripture Desk, and build your Telescopic outline.

Formatting, Highlights & Private Spellcheck

  • Rich Formatting Toolbar: Format your journal live with Bold, Italic, Underline, Headings, Bullet Lists, Undo/Redo, and 5 soft highlight colors (Gold, Green, Blue, Rose, Amber).
  • Private Local Spellcheck (✓ Spellcheck): Toggle ✓ Spellcheck in the formatting bar to underline misspelled words in red using a built-in 55,000+ English & Biblical (KJV) dictionary—100% offline in your browser. Click any underlined word to pick a correction or add it to your personal dictionary.
  • Separate Drafts per Mode: Microscopic, Topical, and Telescopic each maintain their own independent notes draft so switching tabs never overwrites your work.

Share QT & A4 Print Sheets

  • 💬 Share QT: Available in the Notes header, the ⋯ menu, Full Document Sheet, and Archive Rendered View. Converts your headings, *bold*, _italics_, bullet lists, and highlights (rendered as shaded `pills`) into clean, readable WhatsApp & chat messages—no messy raw Markdown symbols.
  • 📖 Full Document Sheet & 🖨️ Print Sheet: Open ⋯ → 📖 Full Document Sheet to review your notes cleanly, or choose 🖨️ Print Sheet / ⋯ → 🖨️ Print / Save PDF (or press Ctrl+P) to print directly onto standard A4 paper with natural multi-page flow and full-page theme background coverage.
Printing Tip for Dark & Sepia Themes: In your browser's Print dialog, open More settings and check Background graphics so the full-bleed Dark or Sepia paper color and highlights print edge-to-edge on every A4 sheet.

Privacy, Multi-Device Sync & Safety Nets

  • 100% Private by Default: Your notes, answers, highlights, and archives live locally in your browser's storage.
  • 📁 Archive & 🕘 Restore Snapshots: Save finished Quiet Times to your Archive tab to revisit later. Automatic rolling snapshots let you recover earlier versions of your Microscopic notes anytime from ⋯ → 🕘 Restore Snapshots.
  • Phone & Laptop Sync (Optional): Open ⋯ → Storage & Sync to generate a private sync key and link your phone and laptop, or export/import an offline JSON backup file.
  • ↺ Reset All vs. 🧹 Hard Reset: Clicking ↺ Reset All inside a study tab resets only that tab's notes, answers, and notes-added Quick Passages (while keeping a snapshot backup). ⋯ → 🧹 Hard Reset wipes all local data on this browser for a completely blank start.
